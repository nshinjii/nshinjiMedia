Privacy policy

What this site collects, why, and what you can do about it. Written in plain English.

Nshinji Media is run by Cole (nshinji), a content creator based in the UK. This site shows my videos, Shorts, streams and socials. It has no accounts or sign-up forms, and it doesn't use advertising cookies. Questions? Email nshinjicontact@protonmail.com.

What I collect

Nothing directly. There are no forms on this site. If you contact me on Discord, YouTube, Twitch or by email, I only get what you send me there.

Services this site uses

  • YouTube thumbnails load from img.youtube.com (Google), so Google sees your IP address when the page loads.
  • Google Fonts serves the font this site uses, which also means Google sees your IP address.
  • The YouTube and Twitch players only load if you pick “Accept all” in cookie settings. Once they load, YouTube (Google) and Twitch (Amazon) can set their own cookies under their own privacy policies. YouTube videos use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com player.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log IP addresses for security.

Cookies and browser storage

  • Required: this site saves your cookie choice in your browser so the banner doesn't keep coming back.
  • Optional: the embedded YouTube and Twitch players, which set their own cookies.

You can change your choice any time using Cookie settings at the bottom of the home page. Without the players, every video still opens on YouTube or Twitch.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask me to:

  • show you the personal information I hold about you
  • correct it if it's wrong
  • delete it
  • stop using it for a particular purpose

Email nshinjicontact@protonmail.com and I'll reply within a month. If you're not happy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the date at the top.
